# Roadmap

## Vision
A single-page portfolio that turns a visitor's "can you automate this?" into a conversation with Ian Muigai, a freelance full-stack developer and automation architect.

**Users:** Small and medium business owners and operators (primary, often non-technical and on mobile), plus referred prospects checking credibility and hiring managers evaluating Ian for a role.

## Current release
**v1.0.0 — Launch**: Make the live site convert: stronger proof, a contact flow that always works, and fast, well-indexed pages.
- Stronger case studies that show outcomes (full-stack + automation + agentic AI working together)
- Reliable contact flow: form delivery and confirmation via Resend, plus WhatsApp/email/LinkedIn channels
- SEO and link previews: metadata, Open Graph card, structured data
- Performance and accessibility at production quality (Core Web Vitals, reduced motion, contrast)
